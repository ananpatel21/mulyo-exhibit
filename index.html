      Space check

      For each station: how deep the object is, how much floor the people using it need, what sits across the aisle, and the walkway left between them. Numbers are the tightest point along each station.

      5′-0″ or more two people pass comfortably 3′-6″ to 5′-0″ fine for one-way flow under 3′-6″ pinch point, people queue in the walkway
      • Assumed depths: a person writing or doing an activity at a wall or table needs 2′-6″; someone lifting the kavad or trying on gear needs 3′-0″; people reading a panel or watching a screen from across the aisle need a 2′-0″ band.
      • ADA requires at least 3′-0″ of clear route; 5′-0″ lets two people pass or a wheelchair turn. The path is one-way, so amber spots work if a guide keeps people moving.
      • Walls are 8′-0″ tall. Panels hang from 0′-11″ to 7′-11″, and header banners sit above the walls from 8′-3″ to 10′-0″. Adult eye height is about 5′-3″; a child’s is about 3′-9″.

      Dimensioned drawings

      The plan shows every wall, object, activity zone and walkway to scale. The section cuts across the room at the Aadar screen, with wall heights and the view distance to the screen.

      Wall schedule

      All walls are 8′-0″ tall, built from 4 × 8 ft flats (2×4 frame on edge with a ½ in plywood face). The 3D model and plan draw them slightly thinner. Header banners add 1′-8½″ above the walls, from 8′-3″ to 10′-0″. W2 and W4 are short returns that frame the entry and exit openings; they can also be a single 2 ft flat or a pipe-and-drape leg.

      Materials and lighting

      Take-home value cards

      One 4 × 6 in card per value, stacked in a rack at the Diya Wall. Visitors take the card for the value they lit. Each carries a one-line story and one small thing to try this week.

      More ideas to layer in

      Media

      QR story videos

      Every panel gets a QR code to its 30-second story video. Visitors watch on their phones, so the room needs only the Aadar screen.

      Kids

      Mushak hunt

      Hide a small Mushak (Ganeshji's mouse) at each station. Kids count them and tell the volunteer at the Diya Wall for a small prize.

      Santosh

      Gratitude counter

      A small chalkboard by the wall: “Leaves added today: ___”, updated each hour. People like seeing the number climb.

      Kshama

      Dissolving-paper option

      Next to the tank, visitors write a grudge on water-soluble paper and drop it in a bowl of water. It disappears in seconds.

      Vivek

      Kids' crossword

      Print a letter-size version of the word search on the back of the passport, so younger visitors can finish it while parents read panels.

      Tyag

      Thank-you cards

      Add a small ledge with cards so visitors can write to the crews who lent the gear. Deliver them, with a signed banner, after Diwali.

      Entrance

      Rangoli threshold

      A walk-on rangoli vinyl at the entrance from Festival marks the shift from celebration to values, and gives a natural photo moment.

      Notes before you build

      • Fire safety. Ask the venue and fire marshal what they require. Gyms usually need flame-retardant plywood or FR paint, clear exits, and exit signs and egress lights left on.
      • Freestanding walls. Gym floors can’t take screws, so perimeter flats stand on jacks with sandbags and the spine stands on T-feet. Have someone with stage-carpentry experience check the bracing before the walls are loaded with TVs and panels.
      • Header banners. The value name reads from across the room. The paragraph text comes out a little under 1 in tall on an 8 ft banner, so it reads from about 8 ft. If it needs to read from farther away, shorten the paragraph or make the banners taller.
      • Values chosen. The eight stations match the packet. Nishtha, Ahimsa and Shuddhi appear on the Modern Day Values mural instead of having their own stations.
      • Section 3 overlap. Several stories (Shiv, Ganeshji, Ram) may also appear in the deity section. Agree on who tells which story before artwork.
      • Volunteers. Plan one guide per aisle to keep people moving at amber spots, start activities, wipe the word search and reset the kavad.
